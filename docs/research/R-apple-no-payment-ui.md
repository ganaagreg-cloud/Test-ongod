## Fact

Assumption: an iOS app that unlocks digital content bought outside the App Store must not show prices, purchase buttons or payment instructions, nor link to external purchase (3.1.1, with possible exceptions in 3.1.3). **TO-VERIFY against the current guidelines before submission**, including any regional exceptions.
