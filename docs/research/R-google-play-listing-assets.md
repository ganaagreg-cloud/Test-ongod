## Fact

- **App icon:** 32-bit PNG with alpha, **512 × 512 px**, at most 1024 KB.
- **Feature graphic:** **1024 × 500 px**, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha. Sources disagree on whether it is required; make one.
- **Phone screenshots:** the Expo guide says **4 to 10**, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha, 9:16, longest side up to 3840 px. Google's own numbers were not read; confirm in the Play Console upload box.
