## Fact

Apps that offer a third-party login (for example Google) must also offer an equivalent login option that is privacy-focused. Sign in with Apple satisfies this requirement.

## How it was checked

Read App Review Guidelines section 4.8 (Login Services) during planning; recorded 2026-10-08.
