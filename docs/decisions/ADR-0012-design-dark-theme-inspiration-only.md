## Context

The client supplied a concept image and pointed to the "Философи" app as a reference.

## Decision

One dark theme (green-black background, gold accent, cream text), floating pill tab bar and a mini player, as specified in [[DESIGN]]. The concept image and the reference app are inspiration only; no logo, colors or layout are copied.

## Alternatives rejected

- **The concept's in-app paywall:** violates store rules ([[ADR-0006-manual-bank-transfer-web-only-payments|ADR-0006]]).
- **Copying the reference app's look:** legal and brand risk ([[DESIGN#Do not]]).

## Consequences

- The admin is the only light surface ([[ADR-0014-admin-light-theme|ADR-0014]]).

## Evidence

- None external.
