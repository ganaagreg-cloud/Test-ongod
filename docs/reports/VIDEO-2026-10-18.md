| Audio for comparison, 96 kbps ([[R-audio-bitrate]], ASSUMPTION) | 29 MB (720p video ≈ 32×) |

## 2. What changes per app (not built)

**API**

- `MediaAsset`: VIDEO + BUNNY_STREAM, `path` = Stream video id, `durationSec` from Bunny; a new upload of either kind replaces the old one after the new one is READY; old Stream video deleted by a job.
- Shared zod: video extensions (e.g. mp4, mov, m4v), `MAX_VIDEO_BYTES` (today audio is 512 MB; video needs more, set after the hosting check), `play` response with `kind`.
- tus upload accepts audio or video and picks the kind from the file.
- Jobs: `media.video-upload` (create Stream video, send file), `media.video-status` (follow encoding to READY / FAILED with Bunny's reason), deletion of replaced videos. Retry as for audio.
- Bunny Stream client and HLS signing (path-style directory token, HS256, [[ADR-0020-bunny-signed-urls-hs256-no-ip|ADR-0020]]); new env `BUNNY_STREAM_TOKEN_KEY` in `.env.example`.
- `POST /episodes/:id/play` returns `{kind, url, expiresAt}`; catalog items carry `mediaKind`; all access rules unchanged.
- Tests: access rules for video play (no access, expired, unregistered device, not ready), URL shape and expiry, replace rule, job retries, with a fake Stream client.

**Admin**

- One media uploader per episode that takes audio or video, with progress and resume (tus, as now), and a confirm before replacing a file.
- Status: uploading → processing (Bunny's encode progress) → ready, or failed with the reason and Retry. Media kind shown in the episode list.
- New texts in `apps/admin/src/i18n/mn.ts`; e2e for a resumed video upload and a failed processing + retry (fake Stream).

**Mobile**

- Install `expo-video` (~57.0.5) and probably `expo-screen-orientation`; a new dev build for gate B.
- Player: for VIDEO the 16:9 picture area becomes the video (`contentType: 'hls'`), same controls (play/pause, −15/+30, speed, scrubber); fullscreen button; landscape in fullscreen (the app is portrait-only today; on iOS the fullscreen orientation option does not exist, [[R-expo-video-sdk57]]).
- Progress: same `PUT /progress` (every 15 s, on pause, seek, end); resume where left off.
- Background (client decides, #17): (a) video pauses when the app leaves the screen (no plugin), or (b) the sound continues with lock-screen controls (`staysActiveInBackground`, `showNowPlayingNotification`, plugin `supportsBackgroundPlayback`), optionally picture-in-picture. Sleep timer and mini player follow that answer.
- States: processing (`MEDIA_NOT_READY`), no access, errors; texts in `apps/mobile/src/i18n/mn.ts`. Still no price, plan or payment text anywhere.
- Optional: block screenshots and recording (`expo-screen-capture`, behaviour TO-VERIFY), username watermark overlay (#20).

**Portal**

- Does not play media ([[SPEC#Surfaces]]): no player. Only the landing copy and sample covers ("аудио, видео") in `apps/portal/src/i18n/mn.ts`, owner to approve.

## 3. Risks

1. **Cost and bandwidth:** delivery dominates (section 1): about $12–119 a month per 100 members at 1–10 videos a week, roughly 32× audio. It grows with members and with the back catalogue. The owner's Bunny account pays ([[ADR-0005-bunny-dev-zone-env-switch|ADR-0005]], #18). Measure one real upload before quoting.
2. **Piracy.** Token auth **stops**: playback without a valid signed URL (not logged in, no access, unregistered device), hotlinking, links after they expire (at most 4 h or the end of access), and old links after access is revoked. It **cannot stop**: a member with access saving the HLS stream while the URL is valid (common download tools read the playlist and segments), sharing a URL for up to 4 h, screen recording, or sharing an account (limited by the 2-device rule). MediaCage Basic (free) raises the bar but its keys travel in the clear; MediaCage Enterprise (Widevine / FairPlay, screen-grab protection) is $99/month plus licences (TO-VERIFY, [[R-bunny-stream]]). Cheap mitigations: shorter video URLs (the app renews them, as for audio), screenshot blocking, a username watermark. The client chooses (#20).
3. **Upload size and time over shared hosting.** A 40-minute source file is probably 0.5–1.5 GB (ASSUMPTION; #19 asks for a sample); at 10 a week that is 5–15 GB a week. One GB takes about 27 min at 5 Mbit/s, 7 min at 20, 3 min at 50 (computed). tus sends small resumable chunks, so a single request stays small, but the host must keep whole files on disk until the job sends them to Bunny, and send them out again.
4. **Does a 40-minute video fit the hosting limits?** **Unknown.** [[R-hosting-limits]] is TO-VERIFY with no facts yet (upload size, timeouts, disk, process limits, idle sleep). Until iTools answers (#23), it cannot be said that it fits. The fallback is a direct browser → Bunny Stream upload ([[ADR-0038-video-episodes-bunny-stream|ADR-0038]] alternatives).
5. **Expo Go limits for testing video** ([[ADR-0034-ios-first-zero-cost-device-gate|ADR-0034]] gate A): expo-video is expected in Expo Go but not checked (#25); foreground HLS, fullscreen and resume can be tested there. Background playback, lock-screen controls and picture-in-picture need config-plugin options, so only a dev build (gate B) can test them. Landscape may be limited by the portrait setting.
6. **Processing time:** "a new episode is playable within minutes" now includes Bunny encoding a 40-minute video (time TO-VERIFY).
7. **Members' mobile data:** a full 720p episode is about 0.9 GB; adaptive streaming drops quality on weak connections.
8. **Demo:** the 30% demo shows locked-screen playback; if video pauses in the background (option a), that demo uses an audio episode.
