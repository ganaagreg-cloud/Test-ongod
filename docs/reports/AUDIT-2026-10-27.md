## Fixed in this session (`2065e58`)

1. **Admin and portal logged people out on any failed refresh.** `refreshSession` cleared the session on every non-200, so a 429 (rate limit), a 5xx or no network signed the owner out. Reloading the admin about 10 times in 15 minutes is enough (the refresh limit, item 2 below). Now only 401/403 ends the session, like the mobile client. Regression tests in `apps/admin/test/session.test.ts` and `apps/portal/test/session.test.ts` (4 of 6 fail on the old code).
2. **Episode description was monospace** (base CSS reset `button, input` only). Now `select` and `textarea` inherit the font too; the admin textarea got vertical padding.
3. **Admin tables hid columns.** `overflow-x: clip` cut "Бүртгүүлсэн" off the Users table at 1440 px and the dates off Контент at 1024 px. The wrap now scrolls; cells use the small size (dates were bigger than titles); episode titles no longer wrap word by word.
4. **Portal still said "bank transfer"** on the landing page ("Хэрхэн ажилладаг вэ" step 2), the plans meta description and the terms, after [[ADR-0046-remove-bank-transfer-path|ADR-0046]]. Now QPay. The landing page no longer shows "TODO(owner)" to visitors.

## Needs a decision (not changed)

### High

1. **Store risk: the app's legal links open the full portal.** Профайл and Welcome open `/terms` and `/privacy` on the portal, whose header has "Эрх авах" and "Бүртгүүлэх" (plans with prices one tap away) and whose terms text explains how to pay. CLAUDE.md forbids links to pay in the apps; Apple 3.1.3 forbids encouraging another purchase method ([[R-apple-no-payment-ui]]). The 2026-10-09 branch `audit-fixes-2026-10-09` (`ef6353d`, never merged) built app-only pages `/app/terms`, `/app/privacy`, `/app/support` in a bare layout; its ADR numbers (0034, 0035) now collide with accepted ADRs. Proposal: port that work under a new ADR.
2. **Push notifications never arrive.** The API sends push on publish, 14 days before expiry and on activation (SPEC D, F, G), but no route stores `Device.pushToken` and the app has no registration, so every push goes nowhere. Planned for gate B (open question #27). The unmerged branch above has `PUT /v1/me/devices/current/push-token`. The owner should know the publish switch does nothing yet.
3. **Refresh rate limit is shared per IP.** `/auth/refresh` uses the auth limiter: 30 per 15 min per IP for everyone behind one address, and 10 per 15 min per device. Every page load and every 15-minute token renewal is a refresh. An office Wi-Fi or a mobile carrier NAT (ASSUMPTION: common in Mongolia, TO-VERIFY) with more than about 8 active members runs out, and then pages fail with 429. Refresh tokens are 256-bit, so guessing is not the risk. Proposal (new ADR, refines [[ADR-0045-audit-hardening-limits-refresh-jobs-payments|ADR-0045]] item 1): refresh counted per device only, with a higher cap.

### Admin (owner)

4. **Dashboard is four numbers on an empty page.** Proposal: money this month (QPay total), latest payments, next scheduled items, failed or stuck uploads, members whose access ends soon, quick actions (new item, grant access). Needs one API addition.
5. **The admin's browser is one of the owner's 2 devices** (SPEC B). With a phone signed in, opening the admin on a second computer asks to remove a device. Device names show "unknown" or a raw user-agent string. Proposal: readable names (Chrome · Windows) now; decide whether admin web sessions count toward the limit (SPEC change).
6. **No phone layout.** At 390 px the sidebar fills the screen above the content. `admin.css` forbids width media queries; a top bar with a drawer needs a new ADR.
7. **Smaller items:** Users has no status or access filter; deleted users show `@deleted-seed_…` instead of "Устгасан хэрэглэгч"; search fields have no icon or placeholder; Ангилал has no cover column; the nav icon for Ангилал is a phone and for Үйлдлийн түүх a slider; "Үнэгүй" is a bare browser checkbox; the episode page shows an empty drop zone even when a file is READY (reads like "no file"); the payment slide-over puts "Хаах" under the title and its badge touches the edge; audit log filters are free text.

### Portal (members)

8. **Looks unfinished next to the app.** Near-black background instead of the forest brand, sans page titles, placeholder sample covers, header links wrap on a phone, no App Store / Google Play links after payment ("Одоо Онгод аппаар нэвтэрч сонсоорой" with nowhere to tap), no FAQ or contact, "Бүртгэл устгах" in gold instead of the danger tone, only the latest request on "Миний бүртгэл" (no payment history). Proposal: three mockups first (as for the admin, [[ADR-0050-admin-dense-forest-sidebar|ADR-0050]]), then build.
9. **Owner content still placeholder (launch blockers):** privacy and terms texts (`[TODO]` on the pages), support email, landing wording, three sample covers. The terms still say only active members can listen (free items exist since [[ADR-0043-allow-free-users-and-free-content|ADR-0043]]).

The app is in good shape: Welcome, Нүүр, Сан (cream), Хадгалсан, Профайл, Тохиргоо, Player and the no-access sheet match [[DESIGN]].

10. Профайл lacks "Тусламж" (help) and the notification switch that DESIGN lists (the bell is open question #27).
11. The Player header reads "ОДОО ТОГЛУУЛЖ БАЙНА" before anything plays and for a member without access.
12. "Шинэ" cards without a picture print the title twice (on the typographic cover and under it).
13. Welcome says "аудио сан"; video episodes exist ([[ADR-0038-video-episodes-bunny-stream|ADR-0038]]). Owner wording.
14. Item 1 (legal links) is the only store risk found in the app.
