# Audit, 2026-11-12: the whole repository, and how close it is to finished

Home: [[00-Index]] · Log: [[2026-11-12]] · Previous: [[AUDIT-2026-11-02]] · Requested by Ganaa ("analyze and audit my repo, I thought it is almost nearing the end; criticize the entire app"). Colours are the client's choice and are not judged here.

## Verdict

**The code is far along. The product is not close to launch.** The API is the strongest part: 681 tests against a real MySQL, access changes in transactions with status preconditions, idempotency keys, one error shape. The admin and portal work. The mobile app has every agreed screen. What is missing is almost all outside the code editor: nothing on the launch checklist is ticked (0 of 26), the legal pages the client is already reading are placeholders, real payments and real phones have barely been exercised, and CI had been failing on `main` for nine days without anyone noticing. Rough split: code about 85 to 90 % of the agreed scope; launch readiness well under half.

## A. Fixed in this session

1. **CI was red on `main` since 2026-11-03.** Three separate causes, all fixed: `apps/api/test/wire-operators.test.ts` used `node:test`, which Vitest cannot load ("No test suite found", so the API job failed although 673 tests passed); `apps/mobile/src/push/push.ts` wrote a ref during render (ESLint `react-hooks/refs`); four files were not formatted (`intro.tsx`, `wire-operators.test.ts`, `00-Index.md`, `open-questions.md`).
2. **[[00-Index]] was behind:** ADR-0085 to ADR-0089 were missing from the decisions table, and [[R-apple-no-payment-ui]] was listed as ASSUMPTION although the note is VERIFIED.
3. The requested features: see [[2026-11-12]] and ADR-0090 to ADR-0095.

## B. Launch blockers, most serious first

**B1. Nobody watches CI.** Seven failed runs in a row went unnoticed, and commit `7f536a8` says "API tests not run locally (no MySQL)". A broken test file reached `main` exactly that way. Until someone looks at the red cross after every push, the 681 tests protect nothing. Suggestion: protect `main` (merge only on green), or at least an email on failure.

**B2. The legal pages are placeholders, and the client is already reading them.** `/terms`, `/privacy` and the app copies show a yellow box "TODO (эзэмшигч): Энэ хуудасны текст түр жишээ юм", "Сүүлд шинэчилсэн: TODO" and `[TODO]` at the end of most paragraphs (`apps/portal/src/i18n/mn.ts`). Both stores require a real privacy policy before submission. This is the owner's text to write, ideally with a lawyer; no code is missing.

**B3. Nothing on the launch checklist is done.** [[LAUNCH_CHECKLIST]]: 26 open, 0 ticked, last updated 2026-10-08. None of the SPEC acceptance tests has recorded evidence: playback under 3 s on mobile data, 100 concurrent streams, a resumed upload, a restored backup, the third device refused on real phones.

**B4. Almost nothing native has been proven on a phone.** [[DEVICE_SMOKE]] itself says "Nothing in the audio rows has ever run on a phone". Today's work adds a native module (screen recording guard) and a native dependency (in-app browser) that were not compiled here. Seven new rows (58 to 64) are in the runbook.

**B5. Store rules are still unknown where it matters most.** 13 research notes are not VERIFIED. The ones that can sink a submission: [[R-google-play-payments]] (never checked), [[R-mongolia-store-payment-rules]] (the owner's claim that in-app payment is fine in Mongolia has no source), the Google half of [[R-store-ugc-rules]], [[R-account-deletion-stores]]. The app now holds three things Apple names in 3.1.1 ("Эрх авах", a gift shop, a code field), all behind one switch. One wrong env value during review and the reviewer sees them.

**B6. Real payments are unproven.** [[R-wire-qpay-gateway]] is TO-VERIFY, and how a member gets from the QPay page to the bank app and back to the app has never been tried on a phone ([[open-questions]] #33). The in-app "Эрх авах" screen is still the test screen of [[ADR-0083-mobile-qpay-deeplink-test-builds-only|ADR-0083]] (`src/testpay`): a plain list and a button, not a designed purchase screen.

**B7. The production server is not in a known state.** From [[2026-11-11]]: the VPS differs from [[DEPLOY]] (systemd service, other env path, password SSH login, which the checklist forbids); a backup has never been restored; a phone sent about 70,000 `PUT /v1/me/push-token` calls in two hours and locked everyone out of login, the cause "NOT proven", and that route still has no limit of its own (it shares the general 300 a minute per address); the account owner's Apple password was shown in a terminal and chat and should be changed.

**B8. There is little to sell yet.** On the VPS 13 of 16 episodes have no media, the three plans and their prices do not exist ([[open-questions]] #30), `SUPPORT_EMAIL` is not set (required for comments in a store build, [[ADR-0062-comments-without-contact-gate|ADR-0062]]).

## C. Risks and technical debt

1. **(Closed by [[ADR-0101-no-server-gate-for-mobile-payment|ADR-0101]]: intentional.)** **The switch gates screens, not the server.** ADR-0083 item 5 promised a server gate (`MOBILE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED`); it was never built, and `IN_APP_PAYMENT` only changes what `/v1/app-config` says. Harmless for money (payment still goes through Wire), but the ADR and the code disagree.
2. **Admins can now create free access codes, with a password as the only lock.** There is no second factor ([[ADR-0040-admin-password-only-no-totp|ADR-0040]]). A stolen admin password is now worth 50 free years per request. Consider OWNER-only for codes, an alert email on `gift.issue`, or bringing back a second factor.
3. **Over-the-air updates can reach builds without today's native parts.** `runtimeVersion` follows the app version, which is still `0.1.0`. Today's code falls back safely when the native parts are missing, but the next change that needs native code should bump the version first.
4. **The screen recording guard has limits** ([[ADR-0094-no-sound-in-screen-recordings|ADR-0094]]): AirPlay screen mirroring to a TV pauses too; Android before 15 cannot detect a recording (the sound is removed, playback does not pause); a second phone still records everything; `isCaptured` is deprecated from iOS 27.
5. **An open gift screen polls Wire every 5 s** per waiting gift (capped at 10 minutes). Fine for a few members; watch it if gifts become popular.
6. **Dead schema is still there:** `PAYMENT_SUBMITTED`, `REJECTED`, `payerNote`, `transferAt`, `proofImagePath`, `submittedAt`, `rejectReason`, `BANK_TRANSFER` ([[SPEC]] says "dropped by a later migration"; it has not come).
7. **The three Playwright suites are not in CI** and need Mailpit, so they rot. Two specs were edited today without being run.
8. **"Views" are members who started an episode**, with no time range ([[ADR-0095-dashboard-top-10-viewed-and-commented|ADR-0095]]). If the client means plays, or "this month", that is new work.
9. **App settings need the database or the server shell:** the minimum app versions are rows in `AppConfig` with no admin screen; the switch is an env value. Deliberate for the switch, awkward for the versions.
10. **Planned but unbuilt UI in DESIGN:** the bell on Нүүр and a notification list (the `Notification` table exists, [[open-questions]] #27), "Тусламж" on Профайл.

## D. Product and UX criticism

1. **The app is a locked door.** Intro, then a login wall: a new person sees no episode, no title, no sample before giving a name, a phone number, an email and a code. The client already asked for browsing before login (2026-11-11, not started). Apple 5.1.1(v), quoted in [[R-apple-no-payment-ui]], also leans this way.
2. **Without access the app is a dead end in a store build.** A paid episode shows "Premium, та багцаа идэвхжүүлнэ үү" and "Эрхээ шалгах", with no word on how. That is the price of the store rules, but a member who has never heard of the portal is stuck. Decide how members learn where to pay (the creator's social pages, an email after sign-up).
3. **Sign-up still asks for more than it uses.** The phone number is not verified and nothing in the app uses it (it appears only in admin search and lists). Every field costs sign-ups; make it optional or drop it. The same for the last name.
4. **Two devices, counted strictly.** A portal login counts as a device ([[open-questions]] #15), and removing one needs the password. A member with a phone, a tablet and one portal visit is locked out and will write to support.
5. **Two front ends for the same things.** Gifts, redeeming and paying now exist on the portal and in the app. Ganaa has said members will not use the portal ([[ADR-0076-in-app-payment-with-bank-deeplinks|ADR-0076]]); every flow kept in both places is tested and maintained twice. Decide what the portal is for (store pages and email links only?) and remove the rest.
6. **Most of the words are drafts.** The intro pages, the landing page, the plan descriptions, the gift texts and today's new texts all wait for the owner (`TODO(owner)` in 13 places in the portal alone). The app reads as unfinished until a person who writes Mongolian for a living has been through it.
7. **Small things seen today:** Профайл shows "0 сонсож дууссан / 0 сонссон цаг" to a new member (an empty state would be kinder); the logout confirmation uses the gold primary button for the action that removes you from the app; the register header's mountain line is redrawn on every step.

## E. Process and documents

1. **Decisions outrun delivery.** 95 ADRs and six audits, while the checklist stays at zero and [[00-Index]] still says "Phase: Build, before the 30% demo". The 30 % milestone in [[approvals]] is "pending" with no date or evidence, though TestFlight build 7 exists.
2. **Scope grows without prices.** In [[changes]], the rename, the intro pages, in-app payment and today's nine items are "Price: not quoted" and done in code. The two paid add-ons say "paid, amount still to be added". Either quote before building or write down that the work is free.
3. **Work goes straight to `main`,** with no review and (see B1) no working gate.
4. **SPEC and DESIGN are behind the code:** six ADRs today carry edits that wait for approval. The longer they wait, the less the "read SPEC and DESIGN first" rule is worth.

## F. What is good and should be kept

- Money and access: one transaction, a status precondition, a lock on the user row, an idempotency key where a retry could double something. Today's gift codes follow the same pattern and passed a parallel-request test.
- The guard tests: no payment words outside two folders in the app, no raw colours or sizes outside `packages/tokens`, every API route documented, the app-only legal pages without links. They caught three of my own mistakes today.
- Shared zod schemas between API, admin, portal and app.
- Honest logs: the daily notes say what was not tested.

## Suggested order of work

1. Keep CI green: look at this branch's run, then protect `main`.
2. Owner: real privacy policy and terms, plan prices, support email, approve the texts.
3. One native build on a real iPhone and a real Android phone; run [[DEVICE_SMOKE]] rows 58 to 64 and the audio rows; fix what breaks.
4. One real QPay payment end to end on a phone, with the switch on, on a test server.
5. Read the Google Play payment and user-content rules and write the two research notes.
6. Server: SSH keys only, backup and one restore, a limit on the push-token route, update [[DEPLOY]].
7. Only then: store submission with the switch off.
